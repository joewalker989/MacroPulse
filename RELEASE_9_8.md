# MacroPulse 9.8 — cooked recipes and pantry-first planning

Roadmap items **21–30** are implemented as reviewed local tools, using existing saved recipes, weighed-ingredient nutrition, pantry, meal calendar and grocery list. No additional provider or retailer integration is claimed.

- Recipe imports offer possible saved-USDA ingredient matches beside the original lines. Users explicitly select each preparation/source before adding it to the existing weighed-ingredient draft. Explicit g/kg/oz/lb quantities can fill grams; household measures remain blank until weighed. Matching never saves a recipe or infers missing nutrients.
- A cooked batch records the original recipe snapshot and weighed finished batch grams. Portions scale the original batch total by cooked weight, retaining detailed nutrient coverage only when its original signature is current. Water/loss changes concentration; actual nutrient retention is not measured. Tiny weighed portions and partial nutrient coverage remain supported.
- Leftover containers reserve grams within the same batch; their weight cannot be logged again from the unreserved balance. Logging a reviewed portion updates the diary and that batch/container together. User-entered storage/use-by labels are retained without claiming food-safety assessment.
- Unconsumed containers can be released to the unreserved batch through preview and confirmation. An unchanged cooked diary portion can be reversed, restoring its grams and retaining the original meal, nutrients, cost and notes in correction history. Changed diary entries require review first. Copied diary meals retain nutrition as a copied snapshot and never pretend to deduct from the original batch.
- Cooking sessions save chosen recipes, dates, planned time and preparation/storage notes. They do not log food or consume pantry stock.
- Price history retains dated per-portion prices, currency, receipt/source and recipe signature. Plans use the latest applicable checked price for the current recipe version. Future, different-currency, missing and old-version prices are excluded rather than treated as zero.
- Ingredient aliases require explicit food/preparation/unit equivalence, a conversion factor and evidence. Groceries consolidate verified aliases and known mass/volume dimensions; unknown household servings remain separate. Existing pantry quantities remain unchanged.
- Household plans rank recipes by pantry shortages and variety. A planning-only stock copy prevents reusing the same pantry quantity on multiple days, and expiration is checked for each planned date. Named household portion weights, minimum variety, a checked-price budget, preparation time and available appliances constrain the preview. Unknown preparation information cannot meet requested limits; unknown prices cannot meet a budget.
- Explicit confirmation saves the reviewed plan to the existing meal calendar. An optional checkbox also adds the previewed missing quantities to the existing grocery list. Plans never log diary food or automatically consume pantry inventory.
- Ingredient substitutions show before/after macros and save a new variation, retaining the original recipe. Old detailed nutrient signatures become stale and remain excluded until recalculated.
- The optional, versioned recipe-tools extension is lazy, retains exact raw recovery before its first write and rejects unsupported future state. Only changed top-level fields are applied; storage errors and stale tabs roll back without changing previous records. Cooked balances/reservations and diary portions require a complete ledger choice during sync. Original photos, live workouts, unknown extensions and offline support remain intact.

Validation: **181 unit tests and 24 browser suites**. Dedicated tests cover snapshots and weight scaling, nutrient freshness, reserved leftovers, corrections/releases, linked data, price/version/currency checks, stock reuse and expiration, unit equivalence, household constraints, retained substitution originals, calendar/grocery integration, import choices, exact raw recovery, storage failure, offline use, stale tabs and narrow layouts. Live deployment evidence is recorded separately.

The full-list execution continues with restaurant items 31–40. Existing Apple/device acceptance and actual AI provider activation remain pending under their stated dependencies.
