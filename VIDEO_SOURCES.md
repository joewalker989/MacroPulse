# Exercise video attribution

`video-catalog.js` records the 78 public wger exercise-video entries retrieved on 2026-10-20, covering 46 named movements. There are 24 H.264 clips and 54 HEVC clips. Browser support varies; HEVC is hidden unless requested and is checked against the device's reported codec support. This is a community collection, not a claim of professional certification or complete coverage of MacroPulse's exercise variations.

Primary metadata endpoints: https://wger.de/api/v2/video/ , https://wger.de/api/v2/exerciseinfo/ and https://wger.de/api/v2/license/ . API guidance: https://github.com/wger-project/docs/blob/master/docs/api/api.rst . Each catalog entry includes its individual source-record URL, author, media license URL, exercise-name author and exercise-name license.

The clips in this snapshot credit Goulart and retain their Creative Commons Attribution ShareAlike 4.0 licenses: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Videos are streamed unchanged from wger after an explicit selection. They are not bundled, transcoded, edited, mirrored, cached for offline use or sold as MacroPulse-produced content. The player displays the selected video's author, license and source before playback.

Exercise-name metadata retains the per-entry author/license recorded in the catalog, including wger.de under Creative Commons Attribution ShareAlike 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . The catalog is an attributed metadata extract; the original MacroPulse player implementation is separate from the licensed media/data.

Playback contacts the publisher and may consume the indicated number of megabytes. No progress photos, health records or diary data are sent. Publisher availability and device codecs can prevent playback. The original offline movement illustrations remain available and are not replaced by this catalog.
