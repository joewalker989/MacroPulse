# MacroPulse 9.9 — sourced restaurant and order review

Roadmap items **31–38 and 40** are implemented with the boundaries below. Item 39, actual AI menu-photo activation and acceptance, remains pending provider credentials and a real consented request.

- Twenty existing common-chain location references now have checked official sources and sourced coordinates. The app filters and sorts them by approximate straight-line radius around a locally chosen area. Source dates and official versus Census/TIGER coordinate methods are visible. The requested private radius was verified separately without publishing the home address or its coordinates. Store opening, driving distance and local item availability are not inferred.
- Users can choose browser device location after its permission prompt, enter coordinates, or search 8,152 licensed offline city centers. Private-area saving/forgetting is explicit. Temporary travel centers and optional temporary restaurant entries remain in memory. Saved restaurants and exact usual orders require separate explicit saves.
- Travel beyond the checked local catalog can open an explicit Maps search link; users can enter a checked restaurant reference and keep unknown coordinates unknown. No live global restaurant directory, automatic Maps import, street-address geocoder or background location feed is integrated. City centers are not presented as restaurant or home positions. See [GEOGRAPHIC_DATA_NOTICE.md](GEOGRAPHIC_DATA_NOTICE.md).
- Menu review exposes source/checked date, serving size, national versus location-specific scope, unknown price, unknown allergens and cross-contact information. Rechecked national Panda/CFA facts retain their standard-recipe scope; local applicability still requires review. Existing older/user-entered records never silently receive a new verification date.
- Item customization retains its original base values and exact modification text. If changed nutrition is unconfirmed, all modified nutrition remains unknown and logging is blocked. A location-specific item requires its matching branch. Sources, portions, sauces, sides and drinks are part of the review.
- Complete orders combine reviewed components and serving quantities, disclose mixed-restaurant scope and compare known macros with remaining targets from logged entries only. The bounded combination tool considers one to three different components from the same chain and ranks transparent normalized macro differences. Missing logs remain unknown; targets are never adjusted.
- Exact usual-order snapshots retain components, source versions, serving sizes and modifications. Loading resets location/price confirmation for a fresh review and does not refresh a saved source silently. Unknown nutrition stays unloggable. Saving a usual order never logs food.
- Prices remain optional and source/date/currency/location specific. Exact receipt totals can be stored and linked to a logged portion's existing meal-cost record. Actual zero remains zero; unknown is not free. A currently advertised Moe's location offer is shown with its source and variant/tax/availability caveat, without inventing its nutritional content or applying it to a custom order.
- Allergen visibility distinguishes listed, may-contain, unknown and cross-contact information. Filters never claim an item is safe or allergen-free; current ingredients and handling need restaurant confirmation. No AI allergen identification or allergy service is integrated.
- New restaurant state is lazy, optional and versioned, with exact prior raw recovery. Failed storage, validation and stale tabs preserve existing records, targets, unknown fields and photos. Order/cost logging commits together. Source browsing, offline area filtering, city search, saved menus and usual-order reuse work without location-service requests.

Validation: **192 unit tests and 25 browser suites**, including sourced geometry, city coverage/license, source/date/version preservation, unknown customization/allergen/price gates, branch matching, bounded combinations, private-area recovery, temporary travel, permission-scoped device-location API, receipt-linked logging, failed writes, offline reuse, stale tabs and narrow layouts. Browser permission tests use isolated synthetic positions; physical iPhone location/permission acceptance is not claimed. Publication hashes and a dedicated live browser run are recorded separately.

Whole-list execution continues with training items 41–50. Existing native Apple, physical-device acceptance and actual AI/provider activation remain pending under their stated dependencies.
